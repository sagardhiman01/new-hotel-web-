Transit Guide

How To Reach Hotel Varn Inn

Effortless access by highway, express train, and flight.

By Road (NH-58)

Directly located on Delhi-Haridwar National Highway 58 near Vedandam Banquet Hall. Seamless smooth expressway access from Delhi NCR (3.5 hours) and Dehradun (1 hour).

By Train (Roorkee Station)

Only 5.5 km (12 mins drive) from Roorkee Railway Station (RK), connected with Vande Bharat Express and Shatabdi Express from New Delhi.

By Air (Dehradun DED)

Jolly Grant Airport Dehradun is approximately 62 km away. Hotel chauffeured luxury airport pickup and drops can be arranged via concierge.
